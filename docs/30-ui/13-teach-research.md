## 核心前提：结论会过时，证据要留底

这篇文档存在的理由，一句话就能说完：防止我们引用一句「大家都这么说」，却说不清是谁说的、原文怎么写。教学盘这个概念区里流行着大量听起来很顺的「业界共识」，但真去查原文，很多结论其实是某一派的实践，有些甚至被摘要读错了。

所以我给 `12`（结论篇）配了这篇底稿：`12` 只留能落代码的结论，这里留原文、出处、抓取日期和可靠度。两者冲突时以这里的原文为准，然后回头改 `12`。这不是洁癖——§2 那四处勘误，每一处都是「读起来很顺、但原文没这么说」，其中一次还被我们写进了设计里。

我特别钉住一条规矩：站点改版或链接失效时，标「已失配」而不是悄悄删掉。删掉之后，下一个人会以为我们根本没查过，于是又把这个结论当共识捡回来。**留着证据，才留得住判断的来路。**

## 1. 逐篇原文

### 1.1 Ludaxis《The Invisible Architecture of Fun》· 可靠度 B

<https://www.ludaxis.io/blog/level-design-puzzle-games-guide>
《The Invisible Architecture of Fun: A Complete Guide to Level Design in Puzzle Games》，2026-02-21，无署名（作者以第一人称自述为 Kalamatic 的设计师，Kalamatic 是一款下载量 500 万以上的单词解谜）。

**（1）第一关的硬规则**

> The cardinal rule: the first level must be impossible to fail. Not easy. Impossible to fail. The player should complete it on their first attempt, in under 30 seconds, and feel clever doing it. This establishes the base expectation: "this game is something I can do."

译：第一关的硬规则是**不可能失败**。不是「容易」，是「不可能失败」。玩家应当在第一次尝试、30 秒内完成，而且觉得自己很聪明。这建立了基本预期：「这个东西我做得到。」

**（2）前五关的微锯齿结构**

> 1. Level 1: Trivial — guaranteed success, instant gratification
> 2. Level 2: Slightly harder — introduces one new concept
> 3. Level 3: Small challenge — combines concepts from levels 1 and 2
> 4. Level 4: Easier again — reinforces what was learned
> 5. Level 5: Satisfying test — makes the player feel ready for the real game

**（3）「隐形」的定义**

> What makes great onboarding "invisible" is the absence of friction. No tutorial popups. No hand-holding text. No forced actions. The best onboarding teaches through level design itself — the level is structured so the correct first move is the obvious first move.

译：好的引导之所以「隐形」，是因为没有摩擦。没有教学弹窗、没有手把手文字、**没有强制动作**。最好的引导靠关卡本身教——关卡被设计成「正确的第一手就是显而易见的第一手」。

> Nintendo has mastered this across decades: the first Goomba in Super Mario Bros teaches jumping without a single word of instruction.

**（4）King 的复杂度阶梯**

> The sawtooth pattern — sometimes called a "complexity staircase" at King or a "ramp-drop" pattern in academic literature — is the single most validated difficulty progression in commercial puzzle design.

> Their "complexity staircase" introduces one new blocker or mechanic per step. Each new element appears in an easy context first, then integrates into harder levels that combine it with previously learned elements.

**它支持**：无失败态、一次一个新概念、盘面自己说话。
**它不支持**：「强制指定动作」。同一节里写着 *No forced actions*，与下一条 King 专利直接相反——见 §3 冲突二。

### 1.2 King 专利 WO2014041203A1 · 可靠度 A

<https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2014041203A1/en>
Google Patents 全文，逐字可核。

**（1）强制手只出现在第 2 关以后，一关一到两记**

> Level two starts off with prompting the player to combine four candies, which results in a striped candy. The player is then further prompted to combine the striped candy with two other candies, which results in the striped candy triggering a line blast. **It is not possible to perform other moves than the ones prompted by the game.** After performing these two moves, the player is told to achieve a certain score within a certain amount of moves.

> Level three starts off with prompting the player to combine five candies in a T-shape or L-shape, which results in a wrapped candy. […] Once again, **the player is unable to perform any other moves than the ones prompted by the game.** Once these two moves are performed, the player can freely choose how to perform moves.

> Level four starts off with prompting the player to combine a wrapped candy and a striped candy that are already placed on the game board at the start. **After this single move, the player is allowed to perform moves freely.**

> Level five starts off with prompting the player to combine five candies in a single line, which results in a colour bomb. After this the player is prompted to combine it with another candy, but the player must use the combination prompted by the game. After this move has been performed, **an instruction is displayed telling the player the different possible combinations of special candies.** However, it does not specify which effects that are generated by these combinations.

> Level six is the first level introducing a new goal for the game. […] The player is then prompted to make a specific move to remove three candies and also three tiles of jelly. **After this single forced move, the player is allowed to perform moves freely.**

**（2）第一关在专利里只有一句话**

> Figure 1 and Figure 2 shows one implementation of the first level in Candy Crush Saga™ where candies have to be matched to complete the level.

**它支持**：前几关「指定动作 + 走对才放开」；一次只加一个新元素（四消 → 五消 → 组合）；规则卡在玩家做过之后再给。
**它不支持**：第一关强制三次组合（**原文没有**，见 §2 勘误 1）；第一关不可能失败（**原文没有**，见 §2 勘误 2）；第 7 关的任何描述（**全文无此段**）。

### 1.3 UserOnboarding.Academy《Candy Crush's onboarding is a total eye-candy》· 可靠度 B

<https://useronboarding.academy/user-onboarding/candy-crush>
2026-10-22 复核；截图 2022-02 入库，站点注明「产品今天可能已不同」。

> Candy Crush onboards new players with an **interactive tutorial** inside its first levels. At the time of the screenshots, a mascot points at the candy to move while a panel above gives the instruction ("Swipe this candy to the left to match 3 of the same!"). The candies involved are highlighted, **the rest of the board dims**, and a "Like this:" box pictures the match. A large pink Skip button stays in the bottom corner throughout.

> **Practice in a real level**: the tutorial runs on an actual board with a score target, so the move you learn is the move that scores, and **there's nothing to fail**.

> **Shown as well as told**: the highlight, the pointing hand and the "Like this:" box all show the same move, so the instruction doesn't rely on reading alone.

> **An obvious way out**: the Skip button is big, bright and always in the same place, so players who already know the game never have to hunt for it.

**它支持**：三通道同说一件事（高亮 / 手指 / 示例框）；**其余盘面压暗**；跳过按钮大、亮、位置固定；教学跑在真盘面上。
**它不支持**：把教学做成独立小游戏（它是同一块盘、同一个计分目标）。这一条我们**没有**照做——见 §3 冲突四。

### 1.4 Puzzlebyrinth《Sokoban, Level 1》· 可靠度 B

<https://puzzlebyrinth.com/articles/stage-sokoban-01>
作者 Toki，2026-09-19。布局经两个开源移植的关卡数据比对，最短解由作者自写的广度优先搜索验证（230 步 / 97 推，约 4366 万状态）。

**（1）这一关教四条规则，全关没有一句教学文字**

> Since this is level 1, every rule it uses appears here for the first time. Yet there is no tutorial text anywhere. **The shape of the level does the teaching.** I count four lessons.
>
> One. **You can push, but you cannot pull.** To bring a box back you have to get around to its far side. A box pushed somewhere you cannot get behind is stuck for good.
>
> Two. **A box in a corner never leaves.** […] Of the 56 floor cells, 15 are cells where placing a box kills the level.
>
> Three. **Boxes side by side cannot be pushed.** […] Two boxes in a line, with a wall running along that line, and neither will ever move again.
>
> Four. **A one-wide corridor carries one box at a time.**

**（2）第一手就是陷阱**

> There are exactly three possible first pushes. […] **(c) kills the level the moment you do it.** […] It is "push but never pull," taught on move one. I confirmed with a search program that no solution exists from that state.

> As a tutorial with no text, that is harsh. **But harsh is how it sticks. That is the design.**

**它支持**：规则可以完全由布局来教；只推不拉这种反直觉规则，用一整关演示比讲一句有效。
**它不支持**：「第一关不可能失败」是行业通例。**它是反例：第 1 关第一手就能把自己玩死，且不可撤销。**

### 1.5 RottenWiFi《Design Game Tutorials for Learning by Experimentation》· 可靠度 B-（内容站，自带免责）

<https://rottenwifi.com/how-to-design-game-tutorials-that-let-players-learn-by-experimenting/>
2026-10-16。文末自陈局限，见本节末。

**（1）迁移挑战——本篇 §1.6 的唯一出处**

> **Add a transfer challenge.** Change the location, timing, object arrangement or objective while preserving the underlying rule. This reveals whether the player learned a mechanic or memorized a sequence.

**（2）该不该教**

> If a mechanic is visible, reversible and easy to test, a long explanation can delay the discovery that makes the game engaging. If the rule is hidden, costly to misunderstand or built from several interacting systems, a little structure prevents confusion and abandonment.

**（3）反馈要指向同一个因**

> **Instrument the consequence.** Check that animation, audio, camera framing and interface feedback point to the same cause. If they disagree, players may form the wrong rule.

**（4）删掉不配打断玩家的东西**

> **Remove anything that does not earn its interruption.** If a message neither changes understanding nor prevents a costly mistake, move it to optional help or cut it.

**（5）四种引导架构（我们属于第二种）**

> | Approach | Useful when | Main risk |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Explicit instruction | A rule is hidden, safety-critical or expensive to discover | Players can follow the script without understanding when to reuse it |
> | Guided problem solving | The mechanic is discoverable but benefits from focus | Players may solve the presented puzzle without generalizing |
> | Productive failure | Failure is cheap and the game's pleasure comes from discovery | Ambiguous feedback can feel random or frustrating |
> | No dedicated tutorial | Core mechanics are simple, visible and reversible | Players who miss one affordance may never recover |

原文强调这是「互斥选项之外的比较」，不是教条。

**自陈局限（原文照抄）**

> Research on tutorials remains limited and heterogeneous. […] These studies provide design questions and comparison methods, not an industry-wide benchmark.

这条很重要：**该文自己说「没有行业统一基准」**。所以本篇任何「业界都这么做」的写法都要降级为「这一派这么做」。

### 1.6 WizusLabs《The first 60 seconds》· 可靠度 C+

<https://wizuslabs.com/blog/the-first-60-seconds-ftue>
WizusLabs Engineering，2026-07-11。文末自陈是「一线的经验与观点，刻意不含任何统计与实证数字」。

**（1）先弹规则页为什么必败**

> Instruction answers questions the player has not asked yet, which is why **a screen of rules delivered before the first move reliably backfires: it is a lecture to someone who came to play.**

**（2）把第一关本身做成教学**

> The design move that works is to build the first level *as* the tutorial, so the player learns the verb by doing it in a space that cannot punish them for not knowing it yet. **A safe room with one obvious thing to interact with teaches more in three seconds than a modal dialog teaches in thirty.**

**（3）静音测试**

> **The tell of a first minute that respects the player is that you could mute every word of on-screen text and they would still know what to do next.** If muting the text strands them, the text was carrying the design, and the design was not doing its job.

**（4）指引靠「会呼吸的东西」，不靠箭头**

> The environment points: a light, a doorway, a lone moving thing in a still frame, **a control that pulses until it is touched**. The player follows attention, not arrows, and comes away believing they figured it out.

**（5）第一手必须有回应**

> What matters is that the game reacts, immediately and generously, so the player's first act is met with a payoff that says *yes, that; do that again*.

**自陈局限（原文照抄）**

> It deliberately contains no statistics, no retention percentages, and no empirical claims — inventing a number to look rigorous would undercut the point of the piece.

**它支持**：句子是字幕不是讲义；呼吸高亮；第一手立刻给反馈（对应我们给教学态补音效）。
**它不支持**：任何「这样能提升留存」的因果说法。它自己都不这么讲。

## 2. 勘误（`12` 上一版摘要读错的四处）

这张表回答的问题是「我们的摘要，哪里把原文读歪了」。它是本篇存在的直接理由——四处都是「读起来很顺、但原文没这么说」，而且每一处都曾经（或差点）被写进设计里。勘误不是挑刺，是防止我们拿一句似是而非的话当地基。

四处都是「读起来很顺、但原文没这么说」。

| # | 上一版 `12` 写的 | 原文实际 | 处理 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 专利：第 1 关「强制玩家做三次指定组合，然后再自由做三次」 | 专利全文对第 1 关只有一句「candies have to be matched to complete the level」。强制手从**第 2 关**开始，且是**一到两记**（L2 两记、L3 两记、L4 一记、L5 一记、L6 一记）；第 7 关没有段落 | `12` §1.2 已按原文重写 |
| 2 | 「Candy Crush 第 1 关官方定位是不可能失败」，并引社区 wiki | 专利没说；Ludaxis 是这么写的，但那是 B 级第三方，不是官方口径。**能确认的是实际关卡符合这条**（6 步内随便凑三消），不能确认的是「官方说的」 | `12` §1.1 改为「Ludaxis 把它写成硬规则，Candy Crush 的实际第 1 关符合」，撤掉社区 wiki 引用 |
| 3 | 「第一关必须不可能失败」当作行业通例 | Sokoban 第 1 关第一手就有一种走法让关卡立刻不可解（作者用搜索程序验证无解）。**这是反例，不是例外** | `12` §1.5 改成并列两派，并写清我们取哪一派、为什么 |
| 4 | 迁移挑战（transfer task）挂在 Ludaxis 名下，写「同上」 | Ludaxis 全文没有这一段。唯一出处是 RottenWiFi 第 7 条 | `12` §1.6 改标 RottenWiFi |

## 3. 冲突与取舍

原文之间并不一致。下面四条是真实分歧，以及本项目站在哪一边。

### 冲突一：第一关该不该能失败

- **不能失败派**（Ludaxis / Candy Crush / WizusLabs 的 "a space that cannot punish them"）
- **能失败派**（Sokoban 第 1 关：第一手就能玩死，「harsh is how it sticks」）

**我们取不能失败派。** 三个理由，按权重：

1. **每一次参悟都付灵力。** 失败已经是正式局明码标价的代价，教学再补一刀等于双重收费。Sokoban 是买断制、无单局成本，它的「严厉」不花钱。
2. **目标是碎片时间的轻度玩家。** 一局几分钟、一天两三次，没有「再试十次也要过」的预期。
3. **我们的教学盘不是真正的第一关**，是插在正式局前面的一小段（1–3 步）。Sokoban 能用 230 步、四个房间教四条规则，我们只有三步的预算——用三步去演示「你会死」，学到的是「别玩」。

代价要写清：**这条取舍让教学盘教不了「失败的形状」**。所以「只能推不能拉」这类会死的规则，我们改成让玩家**看见阻挡**（人站错边推不动，配一句「人站错边推不动」），而不是让他推死一次再复位。

### 冲突二：要不要强制指定动作

- **不强制**（Ludaxis：*No forced actions*；WizusLabs：*The player follows attention, not arrows*）
- **强制**（King 专利：*not possible to perform other moves than the ones prompted*）

**我们站强制一侧，但把强制压到最小。** 具体是：教学态只有一处可操作，点别处**不推进也不记账**，但「跳过指引」从第一帧就在。

这三条合起来才算立场完整——只写「只留一处可操作」会显得我们在跟 Ludaxis 唱反调，实际上分歧只在「默认路径有多硬」。King 的强制是锁死棋盘，我们的强制是「你点别的我不理你」。

### 冲突三：文字到底要不要

WizusLabs 的判据最狠：**静音全部文字，玩家还知道下一步**。按字面执行，教学条应该整条删掉。

**我们没删，但按这条自检**：句子删掉之后，正确的格还在呼吸、其余压暗——这就是「静音测试」在单一动作盘面上的等价物。句子只承担一件事：**说清这一手做完会发生什么**（「点亮正中 · 它和上下左右五格一起翻面」）。它不承担「解释规则」「介绍玩法」。

反过来说：**任何一句话，如果删掉它玩家就不知道点哪，那句话是设计失败的补丁**，该改盘面而不是改文案。

### 冲突四：教学该跑在真盘面上，还是单独造一块盘

UserOnboarding 记录的 Candy Crush 是**同一块盘、同一个计分目标**——"the move you learn is the move that scores"。

**我们做不到，也没照做。** 我们的教学盘是一块独立的小盘（落石甚至只到 10×7），因为它要控制到「只有一条路」。这让「学的手感能不能迁移」变成必须自证的事，而不是自动成立——这正是 `12` §5 要求「两步以上第二步换位置」的原因：**迁移靠换位置来证明，不靠同盘来保证。**
